# Setup (2 minutes)

1. Create a **public** repo named exactly `piyushbl45t` (same as your username).
2. Upload everything in this folder, keeping the structure:
   `README.md`, `assets/`, `scripts/`, `.github/workflows/`
3. Go to the repo's **Actions** tab -> **Update profile stats** -> **Run workflow**.
   (If Actions is disabled, click the green "enable" button first.)
4. After ~30 seconds the cards in `assets/` fill with your real numbers.
   From then on they refresh automatically every 12 hours.

If a refresh ever fails, the previous cards stay in place, so nothing breaks.
